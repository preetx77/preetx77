## 🧠 About Me

I build systems that sit at the intersection of **quantitative finance**, **NLP**, and **real-time data engineering** — then wrap them in clean, usable products.

- 📈 Designing **volatility & sentiment models** — GARCH-X pipelines fused with FinBERT-scored news sentiment
- 🛰️ Building **real-time market surveillance systems** that watch exchange filings and turn insider activity into actionable signals
- 🩺 Shipping **full-stack applications** end-to-end — Flask, React, Streamlit, Flutter, Firebase, computer vision — with real data underneath, not just mockups
- 🔬 Currently exploring: multi-factor scoring engines, regime-switching risk models, and production-grade signal pipelines

### 🛰️ [SGX-Pipeline](https://github.com/preetx77/SGX-Pipeline)
**Real-time insider-trading surveillance system**

A live system that watches Singapore Exchange filings, extracts director dealings from PDFs, classifies transaction types, and pushes buy/sell signals straight to Telegram — with a reverse-engineered auth layer into SGX's internal API (not the public site).

`Python` `SQLite` `Async pipelines` `PDF extraction` `Telegram bot`

### 🥇 [gold-sentiment](https://github.com/preetx77/gold-sentiment)
**NLP-driven gold volatility forecaster**

Fuses **FinBERT**-scored gold news sentiment into a **GARCH(1,1)-X** model as an exogenous regressor to forecast annualised gold futures volatility up to 21 days out — served via a Streamlit dashboard or terminal CLI.

`GARCH-X` `FinBERT` `yfinance` `Streamlit` `Transformers`
